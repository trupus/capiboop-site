What Capiboop does

Capiboop helps you keep track of a recurring injection (for example every 14 days). It remembers when and where you injected, suggests the next injection site, reminds you about your next pen day, bloodwork, doctor visits and prescription refills, and Capi the capybara breathes with you before each injection.

Capiboop is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Always follow the instructions of your doctor and the leaflet of your medication.

Frequently asked questions

I'm not getting reminders.

Open the iPhone Settings app → Notifications → Capiboop and make sure notifications are allowed. In Capiboop, check Settings → Reminders.

I logged an injection by mistake.

Open the Calendar tab, find the entry in the log list and swipe left to delete it, or tap it to edit the date or site.

Where is my data stored? Can I move it to a new phone?

Everything stays on your iPhone. Capiboop has no account and no server. Your entries are included in your normal iPhone backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup), so restoring a backup onto a new phone brings them along.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That removes everything Capiboop stored.
