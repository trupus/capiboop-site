SupportPrivacy Policy

The short version

Capiboop does not collect any data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

To work, Capiboop saves the information you enter, such as your first name or nickname, your medication name, dose and interval, the dates and sites of your injections, notes, bloodwork and doctor visit dates, the number of pens you have, and your reminder settings. This information is stored only locally on your device, inside the app's private storage. The developer never receives it and has no way to access it.

Your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup, if you have it turned on) may include this data, as it does for other apps. Those backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy and your device settings.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally on your iPhone with Apple's notification system. No reminder content is sent to the developer or to any third-party server. You can turn reminders off at any time in Capiboop's settings or in the iPhone Settings app.

Third parties

Capiboop contains no third-party SDKs, analytics, crash reporting or advertising. It does not share or sell any information, because it does not collect any.

Deleting your data

You can edit or delete individual entries inside the app. To delete everything, delete the app from your iPhone.

Health information & children

Capiboop is a personal reminder tool and is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes & contact
